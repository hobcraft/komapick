# KomaPick

Meant to take a photo, ended up with a video? KomaPick is an iPhone app that looks through a video and lines up suggested stills, so you can save the ones you like.

**Your video stays on your iPhone.** All analysis happens on the device. No settings, no account, no ads.

## What it does

- Analyzes the video you choose and lines up suggested best shots (fewer for short videos, up to 12)
- Also works from the Share button in Photos
- Tap a suggestion to enlarge it, then step up to one second before or after it
- Saves to Photos as full-resolution HEIC, one at a time or in a batch
- Carries over the recorded date and location, if the video has them

Suggestions are automatic, so there won't always be one you love. Videos without people, dark videos and very short videos can produce few suggestions, or none.

## Cost

Seeing every suggestion is free, and you can save 3 stills in total for free. A one-time in-app purchase removes the limit. There is no subscription.

## Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26.0 or later.
