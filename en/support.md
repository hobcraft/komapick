# KomaPick Support

Questions, bug reports and requests are all welcome. Please email us.

## Getting in touch

**Email: yumsaki@gol.com**

We reply within a few days. For a bug report, it helps a lot to include:

- which iPhone and iOS version you are using
- the video's length and how it was shot (4K, slow motion, etc., as far as you know)
- whether you chose the video in the app or came from the Share button in Photos
- what you did, and what happened

## Frequently asked

### Is my video sent anywhere?

The app does not send it anywhere. All processing happens on your iPhone. The app contains no networking code and no third-party SDKs. Purchases alone go through the App Store. If you use iCloud Photos, syncing follows your iPhone's settings and is separate from the app.

### Does it change my video?

No. Your video is left as it is. The app only saves the frames you choose as new photos.

### I get few suggestions, or none

Short videos get fewer suggestions, and the maximum is 12. Opening from the Share button in Photos gives fewer than opening in the app.

Videos without people, dark videos and very short videos are hard for the app and may produce none. If you see "No shots to show", try a video where people or things are clearly visible.

### None of the suggestions is quite right

Suggestions are automatic, so there won't always be one you love. Tap a suggestion to enlarge it, then swipe left or right to step up to one second either way and pick a nearby frame.

### It says "Paused"

The search stops if you go to the Home Screen or lock your iPhone, and starts again when you come back, sometimes from the beginning. Please keep the app open until it finishes.

### Can I use long videos?

You can choose videos up to 10 minutes long. If a video is longer, the app tells you. Trim it in the Photos app first.

### The video won't load

Videos in iCloud can take a little while to download. Check your connection and try again. If it still won't open, please try another video.

### The Share screen says "Open the App Instead"

The Share screen has a memory limit, so long videos or high-resolution stills can be too much for it. Open KomaPick and choose the same video with "Choose a Video".

### I can't save to Photos

Saving needs permission to add photos. Turn on Photos access for KomaPick in the Settings app; the "Open Settings" button in the app's message takes you there (the Share screen doesn't show this button). KomaPick only asks to add photos. It never reads your photo library.

### What format are the photos?

HEIC at the video's full resolution, or JPEG when HEIC isn't possible. HDR videos are saved as SDR photos.

### What happens to the date and location?

If the video has them, the photo gets them too, so it sorts into your library where it belongs.

A video with no date gives a photo dated when you saved it. If the video has no location, or you left location out in the options when choosing the video, no location is added.

### What is free?

Seeing every suggestion is free, and you can save 3 stills in total for free. A one-time in-app purchase removes the limit. There is no subscription.

### I can't buy from the Share screen

Purchases are made in the app itself. Open KomaPick to get to the purchase screen. After that, you can save without limit from the Share screen too.

### Can I get my purchase back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?

Yes. Using the same Apple Account, tap the "i" at the top right of the app's first screen to open About, then tap Restore Purchase. Restore Purchase is also on the purchase screen.

### What languages does it support?

English and Japanese, following your iPhone's language.
