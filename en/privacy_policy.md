# KomaPick Privacy Policy

Last updated: [公開日]

KomaPick ("the app") collects no personal information.

## Information collected

The app sends nothing to the developer: no videos or photos, no identifying information, no usage statistics. It contains no networking code and no analytics or advertising SDKs. There is no account to create.

If you have allowed "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings, crash reports may be shared with the developer through Apple when the app crashes. That sharing is an iOS feature, not something the app does. You can change it in the Settings app under Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

## Access to your photo library

The app never asks to read your photo library.

- **Choosing a video**: In the app, you choose a video in a screen provided by iOS (PhotosPicker). iOS sits between the app and your library, and the app receives only the video you choose. When you open the app from the Share button in Photos, it receives only the video you shared.
- **Saving photos**: The app uses permission to add photos (write-only). It adds a new photo only when you tap save, and only the frames you chose.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Videos**: For analysis and saving, the app makes a temporary copy of the video you choose in its temporary folder. Analysis happens only on the device. The copy is deleted when you close the suggestions or choose another video. If a copy is left behind, for example after a forced quit, it is deleted the next time you open the app or its Share screen. Your original video is never changed.
- **Date and location**: The app reads the date and location recorded in the video you choose and writes them to the photos you save. If you left location out in the options when choosing the video, or the video has none, nothing is written. The app never gets your device's current location.
- **Save count and purchase state**: The number of free saves used and whether you have purchased are recorded on your device.

None of this leaves your iPhone, and the developer cannot see any of it.

## In-app purchase

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app never handles payment details such as card numbers. Whether you have purchased is checked using an iOS feature (StoreKit). Apple's privacy policy applies to information about your purchases.

## Sharing with third parties

There is nothing to share, since the app sends no information anywhere.
